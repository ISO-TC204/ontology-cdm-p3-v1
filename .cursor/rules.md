## Sub Specialty

This repository is for the smart city transport service.

## Repository-Specific Instructions

- The transport service area (City Data Model (CDM) Part 3, ISO/IEC 5087-3) consists of multiple topic areas. It is a subset of but is envisioned to eventually cover all concepts contained within the ITS ontology that are relevant to other city services. This potentially includes **all ITS topic areas** (e.g., time, location, regulation, events, vehicles, infrastructure, etc.).
- Relevant ITS standards include: DATEX II, Transmodel, TN-ITS, TMDD, SAE J2735 and similar regional standards
- Reuse concepts defined elsewhere within the ITS Ontology (`its-core`, `its-time`, `its-location`, etc.).
- The full ontology is likely to be divided into an ontology for each topic area. At present, this is the only topic area defined for CDM Part 3
